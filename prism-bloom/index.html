Prism Bloom

Bend the light. Quiet the mind. Move and turn mirrors until every crystal glows in its own colour.

Features

  • 300 levels in 10 chapters, from First Light to Radiance
  • Mix red, green and blue light to make yellow, cyan, magenta and white
  • Mirrors, splitters, tinted glass, gateways and prisms
  • No timer and no lives, with unlimited undo
  • Up to three stars on every level for solving in few moves
  • Hints when you are stuck
  • Plays fully offline, no account or sign-in
  • Sound and vibration can be switched off
  • Free to play, with ads between levels, never during play

How to play

  1. Light sources shine coloured beams across the board.
  2. Drag a mirror to any empty cell. The beams update while you drag.
  3. Tap a mirror to turn it.
  4. Each crystal lights up only when it receives exactly its own colour.
  5. Light every crystal to finish the level. Fewer moves earn more stars.

Support

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game works offline. An internet connection is only used to load ads.

How do hints work?

A hint puts one piece in its correct place. You start with three. When they run out you can watch a short video to get two more. Watching is always optional.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open Settings from the home screen and switch Sound or Vibration off.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. Ads in Prism Bloom are always non-personalised.

How do I reset my progress?

Your progress is stored only on your phone. To start over, go to Android Settings → Apps → Prism Bloom → Storage → Clear data. This can't be undone.
