Stack Grove

Place blocks, clear lines and grow your grove. A relaxing puzzle you can play anywhere, even offline.

Features

  • 60 levels in 6 chapters, from Sprout Hollow to the Ancient Grove
  • Three kinds of goals: score, lines and seed rescue
  • Up to three stars on every level
  • Relaxing Endless mode for high-score chasers
  • Glowing previews show which lines will clear before you drop
  • Combo multipliers and multi-line bonuses
  • Plays fully offline, no account or sign-in
  • Sound and vibration can be switched off
  • Free to play, with ads between games, never during play

How to play

  1. Three pieces wait below the board. Drag any of them onto an empty spot.
  2. Pieces can't be rotated, so choose where they go wisely.
  3. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
  4. Clear several lines at once, or on back-to-back moves, for combo bonuses.
  5. Use all three pieces and you get three fresh ones.

Support

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game works offline. An internet connection is only used to load ads.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open Settings from the home screen and switch Sound or Vibration off.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. Ads in Stack Grove are always non-personalised.

How do I reset my progress?

Your progress is stored only on your phone. To start over, go to Android Settings → Apps → Stack Grove → Storage → Clear data. This can't be undone.
