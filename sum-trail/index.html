Sum Trail

Light the sky, one sum at a time. Draw a trail across the tiles and arrive at the goal with exactly the right number.

Features

  • 120 levels in 6 chapters, from The Lantern to The Dawn Bird
  • Add, subtract, multiply, divide and square your way to the goal
  • A short story: every trail you solve relights a star
  • No timer and no lives in the story, with unlimited undo
  • Up to three stars on every level, and a rank from Stargazer to Genius
  • Rush mode: solve as many trails as you can against the clock
  • Hints when you are stuck
  • Plays fully offline, no account or sign-in
  • Sound, music and vibration can each be switched off
  • Free to play, with ads between levels, never during play

How to play

  1. You start on the white tile, which shows your starting number.
  2. Drag across neighbouring tiles. Each one changes your running total.
  3. You do not have to use every tile. Choosing which ones is the puzzle.
  4. Reach the gold tile with exactly the goal number to finish the level.
  5. A wrong arrival or a hint costs one of the level's three stars.

Support

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The whole game works offline. An internet connection is only used to load ads.

How do hints work?

A hint draws the next correct step of the trail and costs one star on that level. You start with five. You earn one more for every three levels finished with three stars, and three more for finishing a chapter. When they run out you can watch a short video to get two more. Watching is always optional.

Why won't a ÷ tile let me in?

Division tiles only open when your current total divides evenly. For example, 10 can enter ÷2 but not ÷3.

What is Rush mode?

Rush is a timed mode that unlocks after the first chapter. You get 60 seconds, each solved trail adds 6 seconds and each wrong arrival takes 4 away. The story itself never has a timer.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Use the Sound, Music and Vibrate buttons at the bottom of the home screen.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open About → Ad privacy choices from the home screen. Ads in Sum Trail are always non-personalised.

How do I reset my progress?

Your progress is stored only on your phone. To start over, go to Android Settings → Apps → Sum Trail → Storage → Clear data. This can't be undone.
